01 · THE IDEA

Why “You Will Cry First”?

Chinese can feel impossible at the beginning. Tones sound unfamiliar. Characters look like tiny puzzles. Grammar behaves differently. This site turns that feeling into a learning system: small lessons, repeated practice, mistakes, reflection and connection with culture.

Mindset

Stay a beginner

Confusion is not proof that you cannot learn. It is information about what needs another round of practice.

Method

Break it down

Separate sound, tone, character, word, grammar and meaning. Then put the pieces back together.

Practice

Return often

Short, repeated review is more useful than trying to remember everything once.

02 · PINYIN

Pinyin & Pronunciation

Pinyin represents Mandarin pronunciation with Roman letters. Learn the sound system rather than assuming English letters sound exactly the same in Chinese.

Initials

b p m f · d t n l · g k h · j q x · zh ch sh r · z c s

Examples: m in mā, sh in shū, x in xué.

Finals

a o e i u ü · ai ei ao ou · an en ang eng · ia ie iao iu · ua uo uai ui · üe üan ün

Build the syllable by combining an initial and a final when possible.

Sound pairs

sh ≠ x
zh ≠ j
ch ≠ q
r ≠ English r

Lesson 1 — 你好 nǐ hǎo

Chinese Pinyin Meaning
你 nǐ you
好 hǎo good; well
你好 nǐ hǎo hello

Practice

Read aloud slowly: mā · nǐ · hǎo · xué · shū · Zhōngwén.

Then say: Wǒ xué Zhōngwén. — I study Chinese.

03 · TONES

The voice is part of the word.

Mandarin has four main tones plus a neutral tone. Tone changes meaning, so practice listening and producing the pitch movement.

Tone Mark Example Shape
1st ā mā high and level
2nd á má rising
3rd ǎ mǎ low/dipping
4th à mà falling
Neutral a ma light and unstressed

High-value tone practice

mā · má · mǎ · mà

shī · shí · shǐ · shì

bā · bá · bǎ · bà

Practice each set slowly, then mix the order so you recognize tone rather than memorizing a sequence.

Third-tone + third-tone reminder

In connected speech, a third tone before another third tone is generally pronounced with a rising second-tone-like pitch, while its written tone mark remains third tone. Example: 你好 nǐ hǎo is commonly pronounced approximately ní hǎo.

04 · HANZI

Characters are patterns.

Do not treat every character as an isolated picture. Look for components, radicals, structure and recurring shapes.

人
rén

person

A basic character and a common component.

好
hǎo

good

女 + 子 → a useful example of component-based recognition.

明
míng

bright

日 + 月 → two familiar components forming one character.

Basic stroke-order principles

  1. Horizontal before vertical in many common structures.
  2. Top before bottom.
  3. Left before right.
  4. Outside before inside in many enclosing structures.
  5. Close an enclosure after its contents when the character pattern requires it.

Stroke order has exceptions, so learn it character-by-character when you are unsure.

06 · GRAMMAR

Build the sentence.

Mandarin often communicates through clear word order, particles and context. Learn patterns instead of translating word-for-word.

1. Subject + Verb + Object

我学习中文。 Wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén. — I study Chinese.

2. 是 — “to be” for identification

我是学生。 Wǒ shì xuésheng. — I am a student.

3. 有 — “to have / there is”

我有一个朋友。 Wǒ yǒu yí ge péngyou. — I have a friend.

4. 吗 — yes/no question

你是学生吗？ Nǐ shì xuésheng ma? — Are you a student?

5. 不 — basic negation

我不喝咖啡。 Wǒ bù hē kāfēi. — I don't drink coffee.

6. 的 — possession / description

我的书 wǒ de shū — my book.

7. Measure words

一个人 yí ge rén — one person. 本书 běn shū — one book.

8. 了 — completed action / changed state

我吃饭了。 Wǒ chīfàn le. — I have eaten / I ate (context determines the exact interpretation).

9. 过 — past experience

我去过中国。 Wǒ qù guo Zhōngguó. — I have been to China.

10. 在 — location / ongoing action

我在学校学习。 Wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí. — I study at school.

08 · READING

Read small. Understand deeply.

Text 01 — 我的学习

我叫Soad。我是学生。我学习中文。中文很有意思，但是有时候很难。我每天学习一点儿。我喜欢听中文，也喜欢看中国文化。我要继续学习。

Meaning: My name is Soad. I am a student. I study Chinese. Chinese is interesting, but sometimes difficult. I study a little every day. I like listening to Chinese, and I also like learning about Chinese culture. I want to continue studying.

09 · CULTURE

Language doesn't exist alone.

春节 · Chūnjié

Chinese New Year / Spring Festival is associated with family gatherings, traditional foods, decorations and cultural customs. Dates follow the traditional lunisolar calendar.

中秋节 · Zhōngqiū Jié

Mid-Autumn Festival is associated with the full moon, family reunion and mooncakes in many Chinese communities.

茶 · Chá

Tea has a long history in China and is connected with everyday life, hospitality and cultural traditions.

书法 · Shūfǎ

Chinese calligraphy treats writing as both communication and an art form, with attention to brush movement, structure and balance.

饮食文化

Chinese food culture varies greatly by region. Taste, ingredients, climate and local history all shape regional cuisines.

礼貌 · Lǐmào

Politeness and social context matter in Chinese communication. Expressions, titles and forms of address change depending on relationship and situation.
