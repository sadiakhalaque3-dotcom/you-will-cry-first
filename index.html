01 · THE IDEA

Chinese looks frightening before it becomes familiar.

This project began with a simple decision: instead of waiting until Chinese felt easy, I would stay with it while it felt difficult.

Characters, tones, unfamiliar sentence patterns and cultural references slowly stopped looking like a wall. They became a path.

02 · THE JOURNEY

From “I don't understand” to “I can read this.”

01
